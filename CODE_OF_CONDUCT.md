# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PickerMux contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to help
create a welcoming, safe, and productive environment for everyone, regardless
of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving specific, constructive technical feedback;
- accepting responsibility, correcting mistakes, and learning from them;
- respecting privacy, security boundaries, and responsible-disclosure rules;
- keeping discussions focused on improving the project and supporting users.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberately demeaning language;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- pressuring anyone to disclose credentials, private prompts, account data, or
  security-vulnerability details in public;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, release channels, and public spaces where someone is representing
PickerMux.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile,
[`patrickschiller`](https://github.com/patrickschiller). Do not include secrets
or unrelated private information. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially
and promptly as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, issues, or
other content that violates this policy. They may also issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant from
project spaces when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and focused on community safety.
